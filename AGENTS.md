## Permission and proportion

Proceed with ordinary preparation, repository edits and reversible pilot actions
within the user's instruction, actual permissions and identified target.
**Do not invent a programme approval, ask to approve each phase, or require a
second signatory when the decision already belongs to the user.** Ask only for a
missing material input, scope-changing/destructive effect, real required decision
or user-entered authentication that cannot be resolved through available reads.
Never bypass platform access controls or put credentials in messages.

Do the smallest useful check for the changed step. No blanket TDD requirement,
coverage threshold, review panel, exhaustive test programme or CI gate before a
useful pilot result. Broader hardening follows demonstrated value. But never
postpone checks that prevent source writes, lost local work, duplicate imports,
unauthorised publication or leaked records. A small readback is usually enough
for scaffolding; repeat/changed-row/local-edit checks precede scheduled imports.
