## Deliver the quick win

- [~] 1. **Retrieve the actual selected texts and authorities.** Get the current approved local versions and relevant official sources. Record exact scope, source versions and access gaps. Metadata titles alone are insufficient; recheck material source changes before a decision.

- [~] 2. **Prepare a proportionate disposition and package.** Use templates/review-package.md and the existing CHHHS instrument/template. Map necessary controls, propose retain/adopt/convert/combine/retire as appropriate, create draft changes/change log and targeted consultation schedule.

- [~] 3. **Propose the receiving owner and real decision route.** Record content owner/custodian, acceptance/capacity or explicit gap, CMO sponsorship/reserved decisions and required implementation. Do not create a new committee gate or infer ownership transfer from a field.

- [ ] 4. **Obtain the actual readiness check and record progress.** The responsible reviewer checks completeness for the next decision. Until then record DRAFT/AWAITING REVIEW, not checked. Record conditions, effort and next action; final authorisation/publication remain separate events.
