# QW2 — Produce one checked review package

## Outcome
Progress one actual review family to a checked package ready for its next
 required decision. Prefer intern governance/evaluation when its approved texts and
 responsible reviewer are available; choose another bounded case if it is better
 prepared. Work can start from a labelled snapshot and an existing record/folder
 even if QW1 live setup or QW3 scheduling is incomplete.

## Inputs / dependencies
Selected approved texts, governing-source checks and available responsible reviewer. Live synchronisation, AI and CareOps are not dependencies.

## Deliverable and completion
One accountable reviewer has checked the package for its intended next decision, with source/control evidence and unresolved conditions explicit. No claim that a PPG has been approved or implemented.

## Boundaries
Follow ../../workflow.md. Preserve source/local separation, actual authority and
private records. No additional programme approval or mandatory broad test suite.

## Out of scope
Enterprise platforms, integration with CareOps siblings, all-history cleaning,
new clinical publication authority and roadmap implementation.

## Evidence
Observed output, evidence level, private receipt reference, open conditions and
next action. Relevant intended outcomes: O2, O4, O5, O6. No measured benefit is assumed.
