# Local document-control continuity and recovery

Make local working documents, captured reference copies and externally issued policies distinguishable and recoverable without taking control of central document publication or retention policy. Reuse existing edition, archive and lifecycle contracts.

## Requirements and acceptance

Expose links to authoritative publication evidence where established, local draft/review occurrence and prior editions. A source URL or recent file is not currency proof. Unknown or conflicting authority remains visible. Preserve local completion alongside a pending upstream update; prepare but do not transmit a custodian handoff.

Inspect existing versioning, access, visible retention labels and coverage only. No new Purview policy, deletion, record lock, site feature activation or permission change. Document the specific administrative gap rather than claiming a compliant records-management system. Prove recovery using disposable copied fictional fixtures, never restore over an issued or human-edited original. Document current connection ownership/dependencies and recovery steps without transferring credentials.

## Boundaries and eligibility

Follow [the capability assessment](../../../docs/maturity-capability-plan-20261018.md).
The upstream register is externally owned and read-only. Use existing Lists,
library, identities and writers; preserve source fields, human edits and history.
Before native changes recheck account, exact target, licence/action/connection,
environment/DLP, role access and existing notification effects. No purchase,
trial, new consent, permissions, source changes, messaging, assignment, clinical
approval or publication is authorised by this plan. Use a documented native/manual
fallback if a candidate fails eligibility; mark that feature deferred, not delivered.
Keep real records and receipts in approved storage/ignored .local/, not Git.
