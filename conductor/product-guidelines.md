# Product Guidelines

- Show case purpose, source-as-of date, current business state and evidence links.
- Build with existing views, fields, templates and native capabilities. Use the
  retained example rather than performing another substantive policy review.
- Keep imported facts separate from local proposals, responses and decisions.
  During later business use, capture facts once and request only missing inputs
  or actual judgement; that request is not a setup prerequisite.
- Name states plainly: draft, needs evidence, ready for reviewer, checked for next
  decision. Pending/unperformed business states are valid; technical proof must
  not imply clinical safety, readiness or authority.
- Preserve source distinctions and template styles/licences. Use existing policy,
  procedure and disposition examples as workflow demonstrations, not decisions.
- Prefer a short case note and reusable table over another policy, form or app.
- Preserve existing examples and endorsements. Do not create people, assignments,
  agreed dates, consultations, policy judgements or approvals to finish software.
