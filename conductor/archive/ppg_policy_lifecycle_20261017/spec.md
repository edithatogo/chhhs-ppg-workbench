# Full policy-management lifecycle

Implement the ten stages and transition/evidence contract in
[the lifecycle map](../../../docs/policy-lifecycle-implementation.md) within M365/Power Platform.
Reuse the existing catalogue, case queue, evidence library, consultation/readiness/assurance
events and reports. Local validators are preparation, never runtime acceptance.

First slice: fictional initiation and explicit proposed/accepted ownership, scoped by
CaseUID, occurrence, source baseline, document version/hash and expected case ETag.
Prove duplicate/replay refusal, changed-request refusal, stale-update refusal, exact
native readback and recovery. Preserve real cases, catalogue facts and original evidence.
CaseStatus is native free text, not an implicit enumerated transition model.

Full acceptance requires the complete fictional cycle and adverse branches defined
in the map, operator entry, ordinary-user role/access evidence, editable preparation,
version-scoped consultation/readiness/decision/publication/implementation/verification,
supersession or withdrawal and new review occurrence. Existing production generation
and controlled QHEPS publication prerequisites stay open until qualified.
Archived policy/routing conflicts require explicit grounding; do not silently choose
an operative rule. No real approvals, assignments, notifications, issue/circulation,
new consent, spending or permission changes are inferred.
