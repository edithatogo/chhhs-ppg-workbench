This is the single final destination for third-party review, ordinary-user testing,
human usefulness/acceptance and participant-dependent benefit measurements across
this repo. Earlier implementation tracks link here rather than wait for a reviewer.
No participant requests, messages, session changes or tests are dispatched now.

is actually available. Technical agent/code reviews remain part of normal development.

qualification and actual clinical publication authority are not waived or counted as
third-party test passes. They stay with their existing owners. This final track does
not authorise grants, consent, appointments, clinical issuance or third-party contact.
