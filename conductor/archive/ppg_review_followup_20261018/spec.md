# Review follow-up, prioritisation and change candidates

Reduce avoidable waiting in the local review process without controlling the central register. Reuse the existing case and version-linked event model; no parallel task authority.

## Requirements and acceptance

Display accepted versus proposed responsibility, stage-entry date, waiting reason, next action and explicitly agreed target. Capture transitions without rewriting history. Unknown dates and responsibility remain unknown. Human priority rationale may cite clinical significance, incidents or external requirements without importing patient data or computing an automatic clinical decision. A central due date and locally agreed review target remain distinct.

Prove fictional creation, changed-stage history, stale-version refusal, retry, preservation of existing case fields and empty/missing-date behaviour. Follow-up previews must suppress completed, declined, superseded and unresolved-recipient cases, identify unchanged repeated candidates, and show their source freshness. No actual reminders are required for closure. Optional Planner/Approvals/custom-form assessment must end in a justified adopt/defer decision; neither is a completion dependency.

## Boundaries and eligibility

Follow [the capability assessment](../../../docs/maturity-capability-plan-20261018.md).
The upstream register is externally owned and read-only. Use existing Lists,
library, identities and writers; preserve source fields, human edits and history.
Before native changes recheck account, exact target, licence/action/connection,
environment/DLP, role access and existing notification effects. No purchase,
trial, new consent, permissions, source changes, messaging, assignment, clinical
approval or publication is authorised by this plan. Use a documented native/manual
fallback if a candidate fails eligibility; mark that feature deferred, not delivered.
Keep real records and receipts in approved storage/ignored .local/, not Git.
