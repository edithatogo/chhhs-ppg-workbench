# Product Definition

## Outcome and technical completion

Microsoft 365 PPG workbench: source catalogue, local review queue,
evidence/draft library, templates, example packages, protected source refresh and
monthly reporting. Reuse two Lists and the existing library.
Persistent identity, source/local field separation, editable drafts, preservation,
readback and visible failure/recovery are acceptance requirements.

QW1–QW4 are technically delivered: workbench/navigation, review workflow/templates,
safe refresh, and monthly due/overdue reporting. Installation is not clinical assurance,
backlog resolution or measured savings.

## Business work and boundaries

User direction: build systems, not perform policy work.
Use the retained unapproved IETP example or fictional cases; preserve existing work. Actual assessment,
consultation, accepted reviewer/capacity/date, currency, readiness, approval and
implementation remain operational decisions. Existing document-control roles retain
authority. Technical completion never authorises publication, contact or transfer.

The externally owned central register remains read-only. Local progress must not require
upstream replacement, schema changes or writeback. Keep source facts, local work
and externally confirmed publication distinct.

## Delivery scope

Follow the [execution queue](execution-queue.md). Keep qualified operations and existing owners. A custom app, enterprise
model, new site, Dataverse, CareOps or AI is not a prerequisite. Verify exact licences/permissions and retain supported
fallbacks; technical demonstration and measured business benefit remain separate.
