## Data contracts and review behaviour

Standards: framework editions contain issuer, edition, status, source, jurisdiction/service/population applicability. Requirements represent standards/criteria/actions and parent requirements. Many-to-many PPG mappings include document version, rationale, evidence and proposed/reviewed status. Crosswalks are version-specific and may be partial/uncertain. Preserve sourceStandard and standards worksheets; source labels are not automatically accepted local classifications. Seed NSQHS second edition and explicitly identified third-edition draft, including source retrieval/version evidence. Draft requirements never become current accreditation obligations. Additional candidates: Clinical Care Standards, CSCF, clinical governance, relevant accreditation, legislation, directives and professional requirements; load only relevant verified sources.

Relationships are typed and directional: implements, governed by, local variation of, supplements, references, supersedes, proposed consolidation with. Keep category tags separate; support multiple governing documents and upstream/downstream traversal. Reject hierarchy/supersession cycles, allow reciprocal ordinary references. Proposed consolidation links are not decisions.

Roles: PPG responsibility assignment -> stable organisational role -> effective-dated holder appointment. Cover executive accountability, custodian, author, review lead, contributor, subject expert, consumer representative, consultee, endorser, authorising officer, document controller, publisher, implementation lead and assurance/audit reviewer; appropriate committees/groups are supported. Include substantive/acting appointments, scoped delegation, vacancies and accepted handovers. Portfolio movement is a separate effective-dated transfer. Preserve historical actor, role and decision versions. Role mapping grants neither access nor clinical authority. Unknown/overlapping appointments require resolution, never arbitrary selection.

Keep independent state vocabularies for document lifecycle, review progress, approval, publication, responsibility and suggestions, mapped to existing lifecycle events. Use existing states where equivalent. Document any necessary new state and transition; do not introduce one overloaded status field.

## Versioned review criteria and decisions

Each criterion records question, applicability, authority/advisory basis, evidence needed, response options and version. Responses distinguish supported finding, no issue found within checked scope, not applicable with rationale, and unknown/missing evidence. Criteria cover:

- Need/duplication and reuse of existing local/statewide documents.
- Correct instrument: policy, procedure, guideline, protocol, pathway or supporting material.
- Consolidation/splitting and justified geography, population, service or capability variations.
- Legislation, directives, standards, CSCF, funding/drug access, technology and evidence changes.
- Clear actions/accountability/escalation, workforce competence, resources and implementation feasibility.
- Cultural safety, consumer involvement, accessibility, health literacy, human rights and subgroup impacts.
- Evidence quality, conflicting recommendations, incidents/lessons, auditability and measurable outcomes.
- National/state/local priorities, community needs and professional developments.

Refresh retained local management policy/guideline authority and reconcile recorded version/review-interval discrepancies before imposing mandatory rules. Departmental requirements apply only within their verified scope. NHMRC appraisal is proportionate to clinical-guideline content. FNQ HealthPathways is unavailable: record the gap and do not infer its contents.

Decision options: retain, minor revision, major revision, consolidate, split, adopt/adapt external document, change type, propose pathway placement, supersede, withdraw, seek further evidence. Suggestions identify benefits, effort, dependencies, implications and uncertainty. Human decisions and their evidence remain separate from generated recommendations.

Annotations bind document/version and stable section where possible; preserve contributor, date, evidence and original wording. States: received, triaged, deferred, accepted, rejected, incorporated, superseded, with rationale. Carry unresolved annotations into the next review pack; reconcile changed/deleted sections without losing history.

## AI and change surveillance

Separate versioned prompt modules: classification, duplication, document type, external changes, applicability, content quality, implementation. Maintain approved prompt versions and outputs in SharePoint, sanitised definitions in Git. Run records bind document/template/criteria/prompt versions, retrieved evidence, available runtime/model identity, outputs, limitations and human dispositions. Require citations and passage locations; unknown remains unknown. Treat retrieved instructions as source content, not executable commands. No automatic clinical decisions.

Start with manual supported review packs. Native AI requires exact entitlement/capacity/connection/retrieval/permission evidence; historical failures hold only that route. Source registry covers selected official standards, legislation, directives, CSCF, funding, safety notices and evidence. Capture and deduplicate changes into annotations; infer candidate impact through recorded relationships, never automatic applicability decisions. Initially on demand. Scheduling follows a separate eligible task after useful results. No owner notification by default; exceptional alerts need explicitly selected verified relevance/urgency rules, not model confidence alone.

## Acceptance and recovery

- Role succession preserves historical actors; transfers and appointments remain distinct.
- Framework editions coexist without replacing source mappings; cycles and ambiguous identities are handled explicitly.
- Every active catalogue item has a coverage result; every subject element is preserved or explicitly unresolved.
- Changed human drafts are not overwritten; exact retry is duplicate-free; stale versions are refused with recoverable evidence.
- Annotations survive version changes; suggested findings separate evidence/inference/unknown/human decision.
- Exact native schema/output/readback/retry proofs precede deployment claims. Ordinary-user access is verified at later adoption before broader rollout, without requesting third-party review during development.
- No upstream writes, automatic approval/issuance, incidental permission changes or notifications.

Measure reviewer effort, useful accepted suggestions, false positives, unresolved coverage and decision time; baseline and observed benefits remain separate. Update ALM inventory/recovery and publish a successor workflow release only when behaviour changes, preserving frozen v1.0.0. No Dataverse/custom app/new licence dependency.
