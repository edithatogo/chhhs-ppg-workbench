# Catalogue comparison versus supporting archive inventory

The commissioned pilot must account for every active catalogue PPG, compare available
full text and report missing/inaccessible/unreadable/stale coverage. Relevant governing
sources and dependencies also need evidence. An unassociated archive-asset count is
not an active-policy count or an automatic requirement to substantively appraise every
staff leaflet, meeting attachment or source copy before developing the pilot.

Keep all archive sources accounted, without granting review credit or silently
excluding them. Prioritise proving catalogue associations and relevant governing
sources. A missing header or title-only hit proves neither absence nor identity.
Unresolved discovery, supporting-source appraisal and actual catalogue comparison
are separate states. Clinical review/decision and technical retrieval remain separate.

`scripts/ppg_source_association.py::discover_printed_identifier_leads` checks a bounded
retained-text prefix, preserving quoted header assertions and exact text hashes. It
reports case-insensitive ID matches, unique same-family leads, ambiguous identifiers,
numeric collisions across identifier families, unmatched headers and no-header results.
It creates no links, assigns no identity and credits no semantic assessment. A lead
still needs exact source/title/version and catalogue qualification through the existing
protected route. Case-only, date/version and incomplete-header differences remain visible.
