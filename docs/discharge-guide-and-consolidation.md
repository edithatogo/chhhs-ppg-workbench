# Doctor guide and longer-term consolidation

The pilot guide is an unapproved web draft based on historical V6 and an unverified template candidate. Current V7/template authority remains open and nonblocking for drafting. It accompanies, and does not replace, the governing procedure. Patient discharge summaries stay in clinical systems.

## Proposed guide shape

A short checklist: confirm patient/episode and recipients; make actions, responsible clinician and timeframe prominent; give a concise account and relevant results; reconcile medicines and explain changes; identify pending results and follow-up responsibility; communicate with the patient/carer and receiving team; verify distribution and correct material errors. Local timing, channels, exceptions and escalation contacts must be reconciled before operational use.

National reference: [ACSQHC electronic discharge summary guidelines, September 2025](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-11/national-guidelines-for-presentation-of-electronic-discharge-summaries.pdf), especially pages 10,17–18,26. These support prominent actionable recommendations and explicit follow-up; the summary alone does not establish acceptance of responsibility. This is guidance, not proof of local implementation or a new local mandate.

## Consolidation assessment

| Option | Value / effort | Evidence and dependencies | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short procedure plus doctor guide | High / low | Historical subject content and national presentation guidance; reconcile current local rules before use | Implement draft now |
| Shared documentation controls with discharge-specific module | High / medium | Retained release-of-information comparison identifies repeated distribution wording; current documentation/secure-transfer authority needed | Preferred first simplification; cross-reference authoritative controls rather than duplicate |
| Handover/transitions family with discharge and transfer modules | High / medium–high | Conceptual alignment of purpose; complete current handover/transfer texts and workflows not yet established | Preferred architecture to assess; keep destination-specific actions and responsibility acceptance explicit |
| One merged documentation procedure | Uncertain / high | General records controls and discharge clinical handover have different audiences/events | Do not merge wholesale without demonstrated overlap; risks burying doctor actions |
| One merged discharge/transfer procedure | Potentially high / high | Must compare current admission/transfer/discharge definitions, ownership, timing, exceptions and escalation | Conditional; no retirement or supersession decision yet |

Historical audit findings support separating timeliness, content sampling, feedback and executive reporting into a clear audit matrix. Different frequencies can describe different activities; no contradiction is assumed. Existing audit systems should be reused after current-system verification. Administrative episode closure is not necessarily a clinical transfer event.

Coverage remains 985 catalogue identities: 908 metadata-only,76 provisional retained comparisons,1 held. These limits prevent a definitive best-merger claim. Source-only material and identifier variants retain their original uncertainty.

## Decision and implementation sequence

1. Use the change record and doctor-guide draft to collect decisions and missing details.
2. Reconcile current versions, confirm governing documentation/handover/transfer texts and select a small comparison set by recorded relationships. Compare purpose, audience, trigger, action, responsibility, timing, exception and authority side by side.
3. Decide shared versus discharge-specific content; preserve site/population/service variations only where justified. Record retain/adapt/consolidate/type-change decisions and evidence.
4. Simplify the maintained procedure; link each guide section to the accepted procedure edition/section. A source change marks the guide for reconciliation; do not silently regenerate it.
5. Qualify any automated generation individually against edit preservation, links, conditional content, retry and recovery before replacing the manual web route.

No policy is merged, withdrawn, approved or issued by this assessment.
